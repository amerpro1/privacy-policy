Privacy Policy - MultiBox Pro

This page explains how MultiBox Pro handles data, permissions, third-party services, and user responsibility when using the application.

By using MultiBox Pro, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and Terms.

MultiBox Pro is a multi-tool application that provides features such as PDF tools, notes, calculators, conversions, QR tools, solar tools, gold mithqal calculator, AI tools, equation solving, notifications, and other useful utilities.

Data Collection and Use

MultiBox Pro does not sell users' personal data.

The app does not directly request personal information such as full name, address, phone number, or identity documents.

Some technical data may be processed by third-party services used inside the app to provide ads, notifications, AI responses, and app functionality.

Data that may be used

  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob for advertising purposes.
  • FCM Token: Used by Firebase Cloud Messaging and Supabase to send notifications.
  • Device or app information: May be used by third-party services for performance, security, and service improvement.
  • User-entered text: Questions, equations, or prompts entered in AI features may be sent to an external AI service for processing.

Users should not enter passwords, financial information, private documents, or sensitive personal data into AI tools.

Third-Party Services

MultiBox Pro may use third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Google AdMob: To display advertisements.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: To send push notifications.
  • Supabase: To store notification tokens and support notification features.
  • DeepSeek or AI services: To process AI questions and equation-solving requests.

These services may process technical data according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Permissions

The app requests permissions only when needed for specific features.

  • Camera: Used only when the user activates features such as QR scanning or scanning tools.
  • File Access: Used only when the user chooses to open, save, or share files.
  • Internet: Used for ads, AI features, notifications, and external links.
  • Notifications: Used to send updates, alerts, and important app messages.
  • Advertising ID: Used for advertising through Google AdMob.

Permissions are used only for the purpose of operating the selected feature.

Artificial Intelligence

MultiBox Pro provides AI tools to help answer questions, solve equations, and provide general information.

Results may not always be accurate, so important information should be reviewed before relying on it.

Please do not enter personal or sensitive information into AI tools.

Calculations and Tools

The app includes calculation tools such as calculators, conversions, equation solving, solar energy tools, and gold mithqal calculator.

Results are provided for assistance and estimation only, and may vary depending on the data entered by the user.

Important results should be checked with a qualified specialist or trusted source before being used in important decisions.

Gold Mithqal Calculator

The gold mithqal calculator helps users calculate gram price, crafting fees, weight, and final price.

Gold prices and crafting fees may vary depending on the market, location, and time.

The app is only a helper calculation tool and is not an official gold pricing source.

Solar Energy

Solar energy tools inside the app provide approximate calculations to help estimate user needs.

Actual results may vary depending on panels, batteries, inverter, wiring, and installation method.

Before installing any solar energy system, it is recommended to consult a technician or specialist.

Files and PDF

The app allows users to open, view, create, print, and share PDF files based on the user's choice.

The user is responsible for the files they open or share through the app.

Personal PDF files are not uploaded when opened locally inside the app.

Advertisements

MultiBox Pro is a free app supported by Google AdMob ads.

Google AdMob may collect or use technical identifiers, such as Advertising ID, and non-personal data to provide and improve ads according to Google's policies.

Users may control some ad preferences through device settings or Google account settings.

Notifications

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications.

A device notification token may be stored in Supabase only for the purpose of sending notifications.

This token is not used to personally identify the user or sell user data.
